05 / THE JUDGEMENT

Separate the record from the inference.

Compare the evidence together. A supported concern and a proven explanation are different things.

Why not jump to a final finding?

Indicators can support a proportionate restriction and escalation without proving fraud. Release, further verification or another outcome remains dependent on evidence, policy and authority.

Illustrative recommendation

Maintain the justified restriction and escalate with the event timeline, corroborating indicators and unresolved ownership question. Keep assumptions labelled as assumptions.

The situation

A newly created account receives funds and attempts rapid value movement through another route. A risk alert prompts a restriction and review. I would assess the account, payment, verification and activity timeline before recommending an outcome.

01

Alert enters the queue

A new account is flagged after early funding and rapid activity.

What I would check

I check the trigger, account age, timestamps and existing restriction. An alert is a reason to look, not proof of wrongdoing.

Action and record

Record the initial context and maintain a temporary restriction only within the appropriate policy and authority.

02

Build the account picture

The account has limited history and starts transacting shortly after registration.

What I would check

I compare registration details, verification history, device and network signals, and possible account links. I look for consistency and treat weak links as unresolved.

Action and record

Document confirmed inconsistencies and the information still needed. A new account alone does not justify a conclusion.

03

Review the funding

Funds arrive soon after account creation, with ownership still unclear.

What I would check

I review payment ownership, deposit timing, failed attempts and consistency with the account profile. The question is whether the pattern fits a genuine customer journey.

Action and record

Keep any justified payment hold in place while ownership and activity are checked. Record why it is needed.

04

Put the movement in context

Value is moved or withdrawn quickly after funding, with limited platform activity.

What I would check

I compare the sequence, destination, route and genuine use. Timing can strengthen a concern when it agrees with other evidence; it is not a verdict on its own.

Action and record

Document the timeline and assess whether the combined pattern needs further fraud or AML review.

05

Weigh the evidence

Several indicators need to be assessed together.

What I would check

I separate confirmed facts from assumptions. I consider explanations, remaining verification gaps and whether concern exceeds my authority.

Action and record

Choose a proportionate route: release, further verification, continued restriction or escalation. Closure is only an option where evidence, policy and authority support it.

06

Act and leave a clear handoff

The review reaches a decision point.

What I would check

I check the final account state, customer impact, outstanding checks and escalation destination. The next reviewer should not need to reconstruct the reasoning.

Action and record

Record what happened, what was checked, what remains unknown and why the action is proportionate. In this illustration, unresolved ownership and corroborating activity support continued restriction and escalation.

PROPORTIONATE ACTION

The evidence shapes the outcome.

Release

The alert is unsupported after review. Access may be restored within policy.

Verify

Ownership or identity remains unresolved. Request the relevant checks.

Maintain restriction

Supported risk warrants a continued hold while review continues.

Close within authority

Account misuse is supported and closure is permitted by policy.

Escalate

The concern needs fraud, AML or compliance review with a clear evidence summary.

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

Separate facts from assumptions.

Validate the alert, build a timeline, compare signals and explain the decision. A useful case note makes the next person’s job easier.
